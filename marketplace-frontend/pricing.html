Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MarketSync pricing and billing.

What currency is MarketSync billed in?
All prices listed are in Canadian Dollars (CAD) per month unless explicitly stated.
Can I start with one product and upgrade later?
Yes. Start with a standalone product from $19.99/month, then move into MarketSync Marketing, MarketSync Digital, or DealerOS as your needs grow.
How does usage work on Campaigns — Email + SMS?
Campaigns starts at $199 CAD/month for campaign orchestration, segmentation, templates, and automation. Email and SMS message volume is billed transparently by usage.
What is included in DealerOS Complete?
DealerOS Complete is $3,999 CAD/month. It brings Sales, Service, Parts, F&I, Accounting, Marketing, Website, AI ChatBot, and multi-location management together, with generous monthly AI usage included.
