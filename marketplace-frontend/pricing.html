Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MarketSync pricing and billing.

What currency is MarketSync billed in?
All prices listed are in Canadian Dollars (CAD) per month unless explicitly stated.
Can I start with one product and upgrade later?
Yes. MarketSync is built modularly. You can start with a standalone product like Design Studio ($5/mo) or AutoPoster ($19/mo), and upgrade to a Suite or DealerOS whenever your dealership expands.
How does usage work on Campaigns — Email + SMS?
Campaigns starts at $129 CAD/month for campaign orchestration, segmenting, and templates. Email and SMS carrier message volume is billed transparently at usage cost.
What is included in DealerOS Complete?
DealerOS Complete ($3,999 CAD/mo) is our flagship enterprise operating system. It includes all Sales, Service, Parts, F&I, Accounting, Marketing, Website, AI ChatBot, and multi-location group management capabilities with unlimited Intelligence by MarketSync.
