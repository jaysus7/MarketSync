The two numbers

Quality and Evidence Coverage are not the same thing.

Quality is how many of the checks that were actually measured are passing. Evidence Coverage is how much of the picture was measurable at all. Reporting one number instead of two is how a dashboard ends up looking healthy because half of it was never measured.

So a site can sit at Quality 100 with Evidence Coverage well below it. That is an honest result, not a failure: everything measurable is healthy, and something — usually a provider that is not connected — is still missing. Discoverability tells you exactly which, instead of rounding up.

What it watches

Eight pillars, scored separately.

Each pillar is measured on its own evidence, so a strong technical score cannot paper over a pillar nobody has connected yet.

  • Technical SEO — titles, descriptions, canonicals, redirects, broken links and images, indexability, sitemap and robots policy
  • Automotive SEO — VDP and SRP integrity, Vehicle structured data, VIN, price and availability, inventory freshness
  • AEO — whether your site answers the questions buyers actually ask, in content a machine can quote
  • GEO — how AI answer engines describe your dealership, and whether they get the facts right
  • Search intelligence — real query and page performance where a search provider is connected
  • Local visibility — name, address, phone and hours consistency against your public profiles
  • SXO — the conversion path from landing page to lead, measured only where it is genuinely instrumented
  • Validation — whether the fixes you already made are still holding on the live site
What it will not do

It will not make a number up to look better.

A disconnected search provider reads as not connected. A conversion step nobody instrumented reads as not instrumented — never as zero, because zero is a measurement and silence is not. Synthetic and test runs never count toward live evidence. A check is marked not applicable only when the concept genuinely does not apply to your store, never to lift a score.

A figure you cannot trace back to real evidence is worse than no figure at all, because you will plan against it.

Frequently asked questions

What is MarketSync Discoverability?

Discoverability is the part of MarketSync that watches how findable your dealership actually is. It crawls your live public website, records what it really returned, turns problems into findings with evidence attached, and — where a change is safe — applies the fix and re-crawls the public page to prove it worked.

What is the difference between Quality and Evidence Coverage?

Quality is how many of the checks that were actually measured are passing. Evidence Coverage is how much of the picture was measurable at all. A site can sit at Quality 100 with Evidence Coverage well below it, and that is an honest result: it means everything we could measure is healthy and something we could not measure is still missing, usually a provider that is not connected.

Does Discoverability invent rankings or AI citations when a provider is not connected?

No. A disconnected provider is reported as not connected, and an uninstrumented conversion step is reported as not instrumented — never as zero. Synthetic or test runs are never counted as live evidence. A number you cannot trace to real evidence is worse than no number, because you will plan against it.

Does a fix count as done when it is published?

No. Publishing is not proof. Every automated fix creates a validation job that re-crawls the live public page and compares what was expected against what the page actually returns. Only that public evidence resolves a finding, and if the problem comes back later it reopens as a regression rather than a new issue.

Can Discoverability audit a website that MarketSync did not build?

Yes. Monitoring, findings and recommendations work on external dealership websites wherever they are publicly reachable. Automatic fixing needs a supported write path, so on a site MarketSync does not host, changes are handed to you as manual work with the exact evidence attached rather than applied silently.

Is Discoverability sold separately?

No. Discoverability is included with MarketSync SEO, MarketSync Digital and DealerOS Complete. There is no separate Discoverability subscription.
