How it works

Find it. Explain it. Recommend. Execute.

A single, guided loop — and nothing changes in your dealership until you approve it.

Step 1
Find it
Ask a plain-language question and it surfaces what's happening across the store — the units, leads, deals or numbers that matter right now.
Step 2
Explain it
It shows why, grounded in your real data — the aging, the pricing gap, the source, the trend — not a guess.
Step 3
Recommend an action
It proposes a clear, prioritized next step — the reprice target, the customer to call, the task to assign.
Step 4
Execute after approval
On your confirmation it carries the action out inside DealerOS — create the task, message the customer, book the appointment. Never before you approve.
