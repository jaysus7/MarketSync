Everything about what MarketSync is, what it replaces, and how it works.

What is MarketSync?

An all-in-one platform for car dealerships. Three products — DealerOS (a full dealership operating system), Facebook AutoPoster (one-click Marketplace posting), and an AI ChatBot (a 24/7 AI salesperson) — in one login and one database. Enter a vehicle once and everything else happens automatically.

What software does MarketSync replace?

The disconnected stack most dealers juggle: pricing tools like vAuto, CRMs like DealerSocket and VinSolutions, DMS/desking like PBS and DeskIt, website vendors like eDealer, Facebook posting tools like Shiftly, and your service & parts tool — all in one platform.

How much does it cost?

DealerOS: Starter $999, Growth $1,799, Pro $2,499/mo (Pro bundles AutoPoster + AI ChatBot). Standalone: Facebook AutoPoster from $79/mo, AI ChatBot $499/mo. See the pricing page.

Is there a free trial? Do I need a credit card?

Every plan starts with a free 30-day trial, and no credit card is required to begin.

Does it handle service & parts?

Yes — repair orders, a parts stock ledger, technician time tracking, and automatic posting to the accounting engine when an RO is closed.

Does it do accounting & commissions?

Yes — an event-driven double-entry accounting engine, plus a commission engine with pay plans, pay periods, review & approval, employee statements, and provider-neutral payroll (CSV) export.

Can I buy just one product?

Yes. Start with Facebook AutoPoster or the AI ChatBot, and upgrade to DealerOS whenever you're ready — same login, same data.

How long does setup take?

Most stores are live in a day — you're consolidating into one system, not migrating five.

Is my data secure?

Yes — role-based permissions, multi-factor authentication, row-level security on every record, and an immutable audit trail.

CAD or USD?

Both — shown and billed in your currency automatically.
