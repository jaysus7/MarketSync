Questions, answered

The short version.

What does MarketSync replace?

The disconnected stack most dealers pay for separately — inventory pricing tools like vAuto, CRMs like DealerSocket and VinSolutions, DMS/desking like PBS and DeskIt, website vendors like eDealer, and Facebook posting tools like Shiftly — all in one platform and one database.

How much does it cost?

Standalone products range from $19.99 to $599 CAD/mo. Marketing Suites start at $399 CAD/mo, MarketSync Digital is $1,199 CAD/mo, and DealerOS packages range from $1,499 CAD/mo (Core) to $3,999 CAD/mo (Complete).

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every plan starts with a free 30-day trial, and you don't need a credit card to begin.

Does it handle service and parts?

Yes. DealerOS includes repair orders, a parts stock ledger, and technician time tracking that posts to the built-in accounting engine when an RO is closed.

How long does setup take?

Most stores are up and running in a day — you're not migrating five systems, you're consolidating into one.
