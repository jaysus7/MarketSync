By category

Where MarketSync fits against the tools you know.

Each category below groups the tools dealers commonly compare. We describe the MarketSync approach and who each is best suited for — not unverifiable competitor pricing or feature claims.

Operating systems & DMS

Compared with: Tekion, CDK, Reynolds & Reynolds, PBS, Dealertrack, DealerCenter, Frazer.

The MarketSync approach
DealerOS runs CRM, inventory, sales, desking, F&I, service, parts and accounting on one shared customer and vehicle record — and connects directly to marketing and intelligence.
Different architecture
Rather than a DMS plus separate CRM, website and marketing vendors, the operating system, the digital front end and the intelligence layer share one database.
Best suited for
Dealers who want operations, marketing and reporting to stop living in disconnected systems.
Digital & website

Compared with: Dealer.com, DealerOn, Dealer Inspire.

The MarketSync approach
MarketSync Digital is a connected digital department — website, SEO, AI Customer Agent, social, Facebook, video and campaigns sharing your inventory and customer data.
Different architecture
The website and campaigns aren't a separate vendor silo; leads flow into the same record your operations use.
Best suited for
Dealers who want a modern website and marketing that connect to the rest of the store.
AI & engagement

Compared with: Impel, Podium, Fullpath, DriveCentric.

The MarketSync approach
Two distinct products: the AI Customer Agent (external, customer-facing) and Intelligence by MarketSync (internal, management-facing) — both working on your live data.
Different architecture
The AI isn't a bolt-on chat widget; it reads and acts on the same operating record, with actions executed only after approval.
Best suited for
Dealers who want customer-facing AI and internal decision support grounded in their own data.
Inventory & pricing

Compared with: vAuto and other inventory specialists.

The MarketSync approach
Price-to-market, turn rate, aging and market comparisons live inside DealerOS — the same numbers follow the car into merchandising, desking and reporting.
Different architecture
Inventory intelligence isn't a standalone screen you export from; it's part of the operating record.
Best suited for
Dealers who want pricing insight connected to how the car is actually sold.
