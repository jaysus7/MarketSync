Pricing FAQ

Good to know

Is there really no credit card for the trial?

Correct. Every plan starts with a free 30-day trial and no card. You only enter payment when you decide to continue.

Can I start on one product and upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Facebook AutoPoster or the AI ChatBot and move up to DealerOS anytime — same login, same data.

CAD or USD?

Both — pricing is shown and billed in your currency automatically.

What does it replace?

For most stores, one MarketSync subscription replaces vAuto, a CRM, PBS/DeskIt, eDealer and a Facebook posting tool — usually for less than the combined cost.
